# Daily highlights

A day written here replaces the automatic picks on the Highlights tab. One "## yyyy-mm-dd" heading per day, then one
"- " line per highlight, in plain words for players and GMs. "- [Area] text" shows the area and its icon (areas as in
build.py AREAS: Olympiad, Pets & summons, Events, Login & servers, GM tools, Camera & movement, Combat & skills,
Items & inventory, Quests & tutorial, Clan & siege, "Chat, mail & friends", Maps & zones, Client UI, Other).

## 2026-11-14
- [GM tools] New Admin tab in Server Management: shutdown, restart and "shutdown all" (game and login server) with saved countdowns and a double-press safety, plus every GM tool as a button with a tooltip.
- [GM tools] Announcements: log-in announcements now show on the Classic client, can be edited in place, keep their line breaks, and survive a server deploy.
- [Client UI] HTML text boxes can start filled in, so editing a text means changing a letter instead of retyping it.
- [Login & servers] Removed the two test messages every player saw at log-in.

## 2026-11-13
- [Events] New seasonal event: Change of Heart, with Sprockets the Cat in every town, heart pieces dropping from monsters and a rock, paper, scissors prize game.
- [Login & servers] A wrong password or a banned account no longer crashes the client; each shows its proper message.
- [Pets & summons] Pets run, striders can be mounted, Stop and Move to target work, item counts update, and servitor healing shows its numbers.
- [Camera & movement] Tapping W at a wall no longer slides the character, and holding W in the open no longer stops at random.
- [Login & servers] The server list keeps a Classic server in the Classic list while it is loading or down.

## 2026-11-12
- [Combat & skills] Classic critical heals are available behind a switch, with their floating label.
- [Combat & skills] Damage numbers now show on servitors.
- [Items & inventory] A GM's enchant change appears at once in the owner's inventory.
